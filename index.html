Ton Personal Assistant is a self-hosted AI companion accessed through a private Telegram bot. It helps its authorized owner retrieve Gmail messages, stay informed about new mail, manage reviewed email drafts, and interact using text or voice.

Private deployment · Not a public sign-up service

What Ton does

Simple personal workflows, with explicit permission before sensitive actions.

Gmail at your request

Searches inbox messages and retrieves message content using Google OAuth permissions granted by the account owner.

Inbox notifications

Monitors for recent incoming emails and sends selected sender and subject details to the owner’s private Telegram chat.

Reviewed email drafts

Creates and reviews email drafts. Sending is not automatic; the owner must explicitly approve and request sending.

Conversation and voice

Receives private Telegram text and voice messages and can use an AI model to generate responses or transcribe voice.

Self-hosted control

Runs on an owner-controlled server. OAuth credentials are held in restricted local configuration, not published on this website.

Owner-only access

Bot commands are restricted to a previously paired Telegram account. No public user registration is offered.

Ton accesses Google user data solely to provide the functions authorized by the owner. See our Privacy Policy for details about stored data, third-party services and revoking access.
