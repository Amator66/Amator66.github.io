Ton Personal Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Ton Personal Assistant (“Ton”) is a private, owner-operated AI and email assistant. It is not offered for public registration. This policy describes the data Ton accesses through Google and Telegram, how it is used, and how access can be withdrawn.

Google account access and purposes

With the account owner's explicit Google OAuth authorization, Ton requests Gmail read-only, compose and send permissions. These permissions are used to search for and display requested email messages, check for new inbox items, prepare reviewed drafts, and send messages only after the owner explicitly confirms the action.

Ton does not automatically send emails from the owner's account. Access is limited to the Gmail account for which permission was granted.

Data processed

  • Gmail message metadata, including message identifiers, senders, subjects and dates, for searches and new-mail notifications.
  • Email bodies only when requested for reading or when needed for a user-directed draft/review action.
  • OAuth credentials, including a refresh token, stored on the owner-controlled server in a local file with restricted file permissions.
  • Telegram messages, voice notes and the account identifier needed to restrict bot access; minimal operational state and delivery/notification logs.

Data sharing and AI services

The Telegram platform processes messages and notifications routed to the owner's private Telegram chat. Google handles Gmail API and OAuth requests. When the owner uses AI conversation or voice features, input text or audio may be sent to the configured Google Gemini service for generating a response or transcription. Routine Gmail retrieval uses the Gmail API rather than forwarding all messages to an AI model.

Ton does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make it available to unrelated third parties. No email content, Google OAuth credentials or private server configuration is published on this informational website.

Storage and retention

Ton operates on a private server. It stores credentials, bot pairing data, necessary draft/approval records, and lightweight notification state. Email contents are retrieved from Gmail when needed and are not intentionally published or permanently mirrored by this website. Stored records remain until the owner deletes them or the service is decommissioned; some operational logs may be retained for troubleshooting.

Revoking permissions and deletion

The owner may revoke Ton's Google access at any time in their Google Account security settings, under third-party app connections. Revocation stops new authorized Gmail requests. Local credentials and retained app data can also be deleted directly by the server owner. Access to the Telegram bot can be disabled or the bot stopped.

Security

Ton's bot is restricted to the paired owner, and local OAuth credentials use restricted file permissions. No computer system or hosted service can guarantee absolute security. This website does not collect visitor registrations and does not use advertising trackers.

Contact and updates

Questions about this private app may be directed to the developer through the support contact listed in Ton's Google OAuth consent screen. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices materially change.
